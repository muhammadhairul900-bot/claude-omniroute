# PART 2 — NEW COVER BRIEF

## Why a new cover?
The current cover is colourful, but the black-and-white half of the hibiscus is **pixelated**, and on Etsy the cover is shown as a **small square thumbnail**. The new cover must stay clear and readable at 200×200 px.

## Specs
- Size: A4 portrait, **2480 × 3508 px (300 dpi)**
- Etsy photo #1: put the cover on a mockup in a **2000 × 2000 px square** with space around it
- Fonts: **Fredoka** (titles) + **Nunito** (text), the same as inside the book

Keep:
- The **half-coloured / half line-art flower** idea. It shows "this is a colouring book" in one second. Just redraw it cleanly.
- Bright tropical colours (red hibiscus, purple orchid, blue butterfly pea).

Change:
- Title: **"Tropical Flowers"**. For European buyers "tropical" sells better than "Malaysian", and "Malaysia" stays in the subtitle as the unique story.
- Fewer, larger elements so it reads well as a thumbnail.
- Add **Bibi the Bee** so the cover matches the book inside.
