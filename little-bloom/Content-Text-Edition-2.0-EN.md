# PART B — 30 "DID YOU KNOW?" FACTS

> Facts are written for international buyers: Malaysian foods and places are briefly explained.

**1. HIBISCUS** · *In Malaysia: Bunga Raya* · *Hibiscus rosa-sinensis*
💡 The hibiscus is Malaysia's national flower! Its five petals stand for the five principles of the Rukun Negara, Malaysia's national promise.
🎨 red, yellow, leaf green

**2. JASMINE** · *Bunga Melur* · *Jasminum sambac*
💡 This jasmine is the national flower of Indonesia and the Philippines. Its sweet scent is used to make jasmine tea.
🎨 white, cream, green

**3. ORCHID** · *Bunga Orkid* · *Orchidaceae*
💡 There are more than 25,000 kinds of orchids in the world! Vanilla flavour actually comes from the fruit of an orchid.
🎨 purple, pink, yellow, green

**4. ROSE** · *Bunga Ros* · *Rosa*
💡 Roses love cool weather, so in tropical Malaysia they grow best high up in the cool mountains of Cameron Highlands.
🎨 red, pink, dark green

**5. SUNFLOWER** · *Bunga Matahari* · *Helianthus annuus*
💡 A sunflower head is really hundreds of tiny flowers packed together. Its seeds are a crunchy snack!
🎨 yellow, orange, brown, green

**6. BOUGAINVILLEA** · *Bunga Kertas ("paper flower")* · *Bougainvillea*
💡 The bright "petals" are actually special leaves! The real flowers are the tiny white ones in the middle.
🎨 magenta, white, green

**7. FRANGIPANI** · *Bunga Kemboja* · *Plumeria*
💡 Frangipani comes from Central America and is the national flower of Laos. It smells sweetest at night.
🎨 white, yellow, green

**8. WATER LILY** · *Bunga Teratai* · *Nymphaea*  ⚠️ change label from "Lotus" to "Water Lily"
💡 Water lily leaves float flat on the water and have a little notch. It is the national flower of Bangladesh.
🎨 pink, white, yellow, green, water blue

**9. QUEEN OF THE NIGHT** · *Bunga Bakawali* · *Epiphyllum oxypetalum*  ⚠️ change example colour to white
💡 This flower is a cactus! It opens only at night and wilts before the sun comes up.
🎨 white, cream, green

**10. CHAMPACA** · *Bunga Cempaka* · *Magnolia champaca*
💡 Champaca flowers grow on very tall trees, not small bushes. Their lovely scent is used in perfumes.
🎨 yellow, light orange, green

**11. YLANG-YLANG** · *Bunga Kenanga* · *Cananga odorata*
💡 Ylang-ylang flowers start green and turn yellow when they are ready. Their oil is used in famous perfumes around the world.
🎨 yellow, light green, green

**12. SPANISH CHERRY** · *Bunga Tanjung* · *Mimusops elengi*
💡 These tiny star-shaped flowers stay fragrant even after they fall and dry!
🎨 cream, light yellow, green

**13. TORCH GINGER** · *Bunga Kantan* · *Etlingera elatior*
💡 You can eat this flower! In Malaysia it goes into asam laksa, a famous sour fish noodle soup. It belongs to the ginger family.
🎨 red, pink, green

**14. BUTTERFLY PEA** · *Bunga Telang* · *Clitoria ternatea*
💡 In Malaysia this flower colours rice bright blue! Add lemon juice to butterfly pea tea and watch it turn purple like magic. 🧪
🎨 blue, white, yellow, green

**15. LAVENDER** · *Bunga Lavender* · *Lavandula*
💡 Lavender loves cool, sunny places like Provence in France. In hot Malaysia it only grows in the cool highlands.
🎨 purple, lilac, grey-green

**16. CHRYSANTHEMUM** · *Bunga Kekwa* · *Chrysanthemum*
💡 Dried chrysanthemum flowers make chrysanthemum tea, a favourite cold drink in Malaysia!
🎨 yellow, orange, green

**17. TULIP** · *Bunga Tulip* · *Tulipa*
💡 Tulips first came from Central Asia and became famous in the Netherlands. They can't grow naturally in Malaysia's hot weather.
🎨 red, yellow, pink, green

**18. DAFFODIL** · *Bunga Daffodil* · *Narcissus*
💡 The daffodil is the national flower of Wales. Careful: every part of it is poisonous if eaten!
🎨 yellow, orange, green

**19. LILY** · *Bunga Lili* · *Lilium*
💡 Lilies have 6 petals. Lily pollen is very dangerous for cats, so keep lilies away from your kitty! 🐱
🎨 pink, white, yellow, green

**20. DAISY** · *Bunga Daisy* · *Bellis perennis*
💡 "Daisy" comes from "day's eye" because the flower opens in the day and closes at night.
🎨 white, yellow, green

**21. MOSS ROSE** · *Bunga Mawar Jepun* · *Portulaca grandiflora*  ⚠️ same family as no. 22
💡 Its thick little leaves store water, so it survives hot sun and needs very little watering.
🎨 magenta, orange, yellow, green

**22. TEN O'CLOCK FLOWER** · *Bunga Pukul Sepuluh* · *Portulaca*  ⚠️ same family as no. 21
💡 It got its name because it opens at around 10 o'clock in the morning when the sun is bright, then closes in the afternoon. ⏰
🎨 pink, yellow, green

**23. GOLDEN TRUMPET** · *Bunga Alamanda* · *Allamanda cathartica*
💡 Its flowers are shaped like a trumpet. Its sap is poisonous, so look but never taste!
🎨 yellow, light orange, green

**24. CANNA LILY** · *Bunga Tasbih* · *Canna indica*  ⚠️ redraw
💡 Its round, hard, black seeds were once used to make prayer beads. That's how it got its Malay name, which means "prayer bead flower"!
🎨 red, orange, yellow, green

**25. COLEUS** · *Pokok Ati-ati* · *Coleus scutellarioides*
💡 Coleus is famous for its colourful leaves, not its flowers. Its real flowers are tiny and bluish-purple.
🎨 magenta, lime green, purple

**26. IXORA** · *Bunga Jejarum ("needle flower")* · *Ixora*  ⚠️ change title from "Kacapiring"
💡 Each tiny flower has a long, thin tube like a needle, and one cluster can hold dozens of little flowers!
🎨 red, orange, green

**27. CROWN OF THORNS** · *Bunga Janda Kaya* · *Euphorbia milii*  ⚠️ check the local name
💡 The red parts are special leaves, not petals. The stem is covered in thorns and its white sap can sting your skin.
🎨 red, green, brown

**28. RAFFLESIA** · *Bunga Pakma* · *Rafflesia*  (replaces "Euphorbia")
💡 Rafflesia is the biggest single flower in the world, almost 1 metre (3 feet) wide! It has no leaves, stem or roots, and it smells like rotting meat to attract flies.
🎨 brick red, white spots, brown

**29. SACRED LOTUS** · *Bunga Seroja* · *Nelumbo nucifera*
💡 Lotus leaves are waterproof: water drops roll off and carry the dirt away! Its seeds are made into the sweet filling of mooncakes.
🎨 pink, yellow, green

**30. PERIWINKLE** · *Bunga Tapak Dara* · *Catharanthus roseus*  (replaces "Kembang Sepatu")
💡 Scientists use substances from this little flower to make cancer medicine. But never eat it, because it's poisonous!
🎨 pink, white, green

### Spare (to replace Lavender / Tulip / Daffodil / one Portulaca if you want a 100% tropical book)

**LOBSTER CLAW** · *Bunga Sepit Udang* · *Heliconia*
💡 It looks just like a lobster's claw! It comes from the rainforests of Central and South America, where hummingbirds sip nectar from inside the "claws".
🎨 red, yellow, green
