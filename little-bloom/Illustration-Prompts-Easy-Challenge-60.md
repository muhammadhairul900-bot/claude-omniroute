## How to get consistent, print-ready results

**Settings**
- Aspect ratio **portrait 2:3** (or 1:1.41 for A4 if your tool allows)
- Highest resolution available (at least 2000 px tall)
- Generate 3–4 versions and pick the best one

**Every prompt ends with the same "style tail"**, so all 60 pages match:
- Easy: *"…very few details, extra-thick bold black outlines, big simple closed shapes that are easy to colour, no tiny parts, no patterns, pure white background, no shading, no grey, no text, centred, cute cartoon style"*
- Challenge: *"…thin clean black outlines, every shape closed, pure white background, no shading, no grey, no text, fills the whole page, portrait"*

**Check each image before using it**
- [ ] Lines are pure black and the background is pure white (no grey shading)
- [ ] Every shape is closed, so crayons and digital fill work
- [ ] No text, letters, signatures or watermarks
- [ ] The flower looks like the real flower (compare with the colour example on the Medium page)
- [ ] Easy: shapes big enough for a 3-year-old · Challenge: enough detail to keep a 9-year-old busy for 20+ minutes

**File names** (so I can drop them straight into the book):
`easy_01_hibiscus.png` … `easy_30_periwinkle.png` and `challenge_01_hibiscus.png` … `challenge_30_periwinkle.png`

**If the AI keeps adding grey shading**, add: *"line art only, like a printed colouring book, black ink on white paper"*.
**If the AI adds text**, add: *"absolutely no words or letters anywhere"*.
