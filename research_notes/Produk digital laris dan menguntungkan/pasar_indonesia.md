# Selling Digital Products from Indonesia (2025-2026): Domestic Market and Global Platforms

Research date: 28 Nov 2026. Sources are mostly 2025-2026; older items are flagged.

## 1. Which digital products sell well to Indonesian buyers, at what Rupiah prices, and what is the evidence of demand?

### Takeaway
Templates (CV/ATS, Canva, Notion/spreadsheet planners), digital wedding invitations, e-books, online classes, and photo presets keep showing up in Indonesian 2025-2026 "best-selling" lists. Prices are low: Rp5.000-Rp150.000 for templates, and roughly Rp39.000-Rp150.000 for template-based wedding invitation websites, while custom designs cost Rp500.000-Rp2 juta. The demand evidence is mostly qualitative, from blogs, creator posts and marketplace listings. I found no hard sales-volume data.

### Cited Findings
- **Templates and planners:** Tempo Institute (April 2026) says spreadsheet planners and design templates for Canva and Notion are among the most-searched digital products on Lynk.id. It estimates margins of 70-95%. Its list also includes e-books, online courses, printables, digital art, music/SFX, photo presets ("among the most searched digital products in Indonesia"), stock photos, video assets/LUTs and digital services. Note: the article attributes part of its list to Sellfy, a global source. — [Tempo Institute Blog](https://blog.tempoinstitute.com/berita/produk-digital-yang-paling-banyak-dicari)
- **Wedding and event invitations:** UKM Indonesia ("15 Produk Digital Terlaris di Tahun 2025") calls digital invitation templates one of the best-selling digital products, used for weddings, seminars, birthdays and other events. — [ukmindonesia.id](https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/15-produk-digital-terlaris-di-tahun-2025-yang-banyak-dicari-netizen-)
- **Common product lists:** A Lemon8 creator post ("7 Produk Digital Paling Dicari dan Laris di 2025") lists CV templates, planners and wedding invitations, and claims $100-$1000/month potential. This is unverified creator content. — [Lemon8](https://www.lemon8-app.com/experience/produk-digital-paling-dicari?region=id)
- **Prices: CV / ATS templates**
  - A Lynk.id store sells a "Template CV ATS" for IDR 5,000. — [lynk.id/aestydigital](https://lynk.id/aestydigital/7jggdd0k40nr)
  - Shopee has a Canva ATS-friendly CV template listing at Rp65.000. — [Shopee](https://shopee.co.id/Template-cv-ats-template-cv-canva-profesional-Desain-CV-Menarik-Ats-Friendly-Curriculum-Vitae-i.1556886622.26387724607)

  - YouTube and TikTok tutorials on making ATS CV templates in Canva and selling them on Lynk.id or Shopee are common. This suggests a crowded, low-barrier niche. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfQOcc2vwrw); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5V-7MoK2Mwo)

  - Indoinvite: from Rp39.000, with no expiry period. — [indoinvite.com](https://indoinvite.com/harga)
  - Seremoni.id: Basic free, Premium Rp79.000, Platinum Rp149.000. — [seremoni.id](https://seremoni.id/)
  - Menica.pro (2026): Premium Rp79.000, discounted to Rp49.000. A custom domain adds Rp250.000 and a custom design adds Rp500.000. Menica claims 12,000+ users. — [menica.pro](https://menica.pro/harga-paket)
  - Tokopedia listings run from Rp60.000 to Rp99.999. — [Tokopedia](https://www.tokopedia.com/find/undangan-digital-pernikahan-website?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=find)
  - A vendor price list (2025) shows template design at Rp650.000 and custom designs from Rp1.500.000 to Rp1.950.000. — [Scribd pricelist](https://id.scribd.com/document/948602449/Pricelist)
  - Printing invitations for 500 guests can cost at least Rp2.500.000, against Rp45.000 for a basic digital invitation. That gap is the cost-saving pitch. — [amanahtransporter.com](https://amanahtransporter.com/undangan-digital-wedding-invitation/)
- **Lynk.id creator testimonials:** Lynk.id's homepage quotes education creators such as @se.kelas and @filosofiexcel (Excel classes) saying they sell digital products and classes there. This points to Excel and skills classes as a working niche. — [lynk.id](https://lynk.id/)
- **Teacher products:** Teachers are encouraged to sell LKS/LKPD, teaching media, e-books and modules, and worksheets. The evidence is social posts only, with no prices found. — [Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/tuti.rl/posts/guru-bisa-cuan-dari-produk-digital-banyak-guru-belum-sadar-gara-gara-bangun-pers/10229256117019023/); [Threads](https://www.threads.com/@bapack2_kreator/post/DI57-YbSRo5/10-ide-produk-digital-di-kalangan-guru?hl=en)

### Inferences
- The domestic market is high-volume and low-price. A typical template sells for Rp10.000-Rp75.000. Because PLR resellers push prices down toward Rp5.000, differentiation matters a lot. Examples: niche-specific templates, bundled guides, or wedding invitation SaaS with features.
- Wedding invitations sell best as a *service/SaaS* (a hosted website with RSVP, music and a gift feature) rather than as a downloadable file. That model may need more technical setup than a plain template.
- Exam-prep (CPNS/UTBK) and teaching modules are commonly cited, but I found no reliable price or volume data in this pass.

### Gaps
- I found no hard data (GMV, unit sales, Google Trends numbers) for any product category. Evidence is listings and blog lists.
- Price points for soal CPNS/UTBK packages, preset Lightroom, Excel report templates, and modul ajar guru were not verified in this pass.
- There are no official Shopee/Tokopedia statistics on digital-product categories.

## 2. Local platforms for selling digital products, and their fees

### Takeaway
Lynk.id, Mayar, Trakteer and KaryaKarsa all take a percentage cut, from about 3% to 10%, plus payment-channel or withdrawal fees. Mayar is the cheapest on paper for digital downloads at 3-4% plus the channel fee, and it can pass both fees to the buyer. Lynk.id's free plan costs 5% plus Rp5k per withdrawal. KaryaKarsa takes 10%. Marketplaces (Shopee/Tokopedia) add tax withholding from Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Lynk.id (official pricing page, Nov 2026)**
  - Basic (free): 5% transaction fee (shown as reduced from 10%), Rp5k withdrawal fee, 10 minutes of e-course video.
  - PRO (Rp99k/month): 3% transaction fee, free withdrawal, 480 minutes of e-course video, automatic PDF watermark, WhatsApp broadcast, Meta CAPI.
  - Brandpreneur: up to 1%.
  - Prices exclude PPN. — [lynk.id/pricing](https://lynk.id/pricing)
  - The minimum withdrawal is Rp100.000. — [lynk.id/terms](https://lynk.id/terms)
  - By default, the payment-method "convenience fee" is paid by the buyer, and the seller can change this. — [Lynk FAQ](https://lynk.id/faq/detail/686a7c52fb316faca878ff74-1585-85135)
- **Mayar (docs, last modified Aug 2025)**
  - Admin fee for Digital Download / E-Book / Podcast / Audiobook: 4% on Starter, 3% on Business, 0% on Enterprise.
  - Online classes / bootcamps: 4% / 3%.
  - Webinars / events / coaching: 3% / 2%.
  - Channel fees: QRIS 0.7%, e-wallet 1.5%, VA Rp4.000, credit card 2.6% + Rp2.000, minimarket Rp5.000-7.500.
  - Flat withdrawal fee: Rp2.775.
  - Both the channel fee and the admin fee can be passed to the customer.
  - Mayar acts as Merchant of Record for digital goods. — [docs.mayar.id](https://docs.mayar.id/mor/fees)
- **Trakteer:** platform fee of 5% of each support/donation. It has a "PG Fee by Supporter" option that moves the payment-gateway fee to the supporter. — [Trakteer pricing](https://trakteer.id/feature-and-pricing); [Trakteer Help](https://help.trakteer.id/help-center/articles/3/berapa-biaya-yang-dikenakan-trakteer); [Instagram @trakteer](https://www.instagram.com/p/DKrK39QykLp/)
- **KaryaKarsa:** the creator gets 90% of each transaction, before bank transfer fees and any applicable tax. KaryaKarsa takes 10%, which includes transaction costs. — [KaryaKarsa Help](https://help.karyakarsa.com/panduan-kreator/kebijakan-mengenai-gajiandikaryakarsa)
- **Creator fee comparisons:** A creator comparing withdrawal fees found Mayar at Rp2.775 flat versus Lynk at 5% of the amount. Note: Lynk's official page now says Rp5k flat on Basic, so the Threads figure may be outdated or may mix in the transaction fee. — [Threads @bapakkerjaremote](https://www.threads.com/@bapakkerjaremote/post/DDvwQ2FS-p9?hl=en); contrast with [lynk.id/pricing](https://lynk.id/pricing)
- **Shopee/Tokopedia:** digital templates are sold there; CV and wedding-invitation listings were found (see Section 1). From 1 Nov 2026, marketplaces (Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada, Blibli) withhold PPh Pasal 22 at 0.5% of gross sales from domestic sellers before disbursing funds. This is regulated by PMK 37/2025. Sellers with annual turnover up to Rp500 juta can avoid the withholding by submitting a statement letter. — [SWS Consulting](https://swsconsulting.co.id/pajak-marketplace-mulai-1-november-2026-tarif-05-siapa-yang-kena-dan-cara-kerjanya/); [Ortax](https://ortax.org/omzet-tembus-rp-500-juta-marketplace-bisa-otomatis-potong-pajak-pedagang-0-5-persen)
- **Lynk.id positioning:** Lynk.id grew from a link-in-bio tool into a micro-storefront for local creators. A reviewer recommends it for starting out and validating ideas (review updated July 2025). — [SoftwareSeni](https://www.softwareseni.co.id/blog/review-lynk-id)

### Inferences
- For a digital download at Rp50.000:
  - Lynk Basic: about Rp2.500 fee, plus Rp5k per withdrawal.
  - Mayar Starter: Rp2.000 admin fee plus Rp350 channel fee if paid by QRIS, plus Rp2.775 per withdrawal.
  - KaryaKarsa: Rp5.000.
  - Because Mayar and Lynk let the buyer pay the channel/convenience fee, the effective take for sellers is low, often under 5%.
- Typical funnel: traffic from Instagram/TikTok content goes to a link-in-bio storefront (Lynk/Mayar). That pattern dominates the tutorials found.

### Gaps
- I could not verify Shopee and Tokopedia/TikTok Shop's current admin fees or their policies for the digital-goods category. TikTok Shop's rules on downloadable digital products are unclear.
- I did not research MySkill-style course platforms' revenue-share terms.
- I found no data on how many Indonesians use Gumroad to sell to *domestic* buyers.

## 3. Global platforms: Etsy and Gumroad setup, payouts, and FX for Indonesian individuals

### Takeaway
**Etsy:** Indonesia is on Etsy's Etsy Payments list without the Payoneer asterisk, so an Indonesian individual can open a shop and get paid directly to a local bank in IDR. The Indonesia-specific costs are:
- payment processing of 4.5% + Rp7.000 per order
- a Rp28.000 deposit fee on payouts below Rp1.400.000
- 2.5% currency conversion if the listing currency differs from IDR
- VAT charged on Etsy fees regardless of tax ID
- the standard 6.5% transaction fee

**Gumroad:** pays Indonesian creators by bank deposit in IDR. PayPal payouts are only for countries without bank support, and an Indonesian user reported PayPal was removed for them in late 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **Etsy eligibility:** Indonesia is listed among the countries eligible for Etsy Payments. — [Etsy Help: Countries Eligible for Etsy Payments](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015710408-Countries-Eligible-for-Etsy-Payments)
  - Etsy's Payments Policy (version effective until 9 Oct 2025) lists Indonesia and gives Indonesian bank-account processing fees of 4.5% + 7,000 IDR, plus a deposit fee of 28,000 IDR for deposits under 1,400,000 IDR. — [Etsy Payments Policy (until Oct 9, 2025)](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/etsy-payments-policy-effective-until/1396469674685)
  - The current help page still shows the IDR 28,000 / 1,400,000 / 28,000 deposit figures for Indonesia. — [Etsy Help: What is Etsy Payments](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002120927-What-is-Etsy-Payments)
- **Etsy requirements and payouts** (secondary summary of Etsy help pages, published Oct 2026, updated Nov 2026) — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/sell-on-etsy-from-indonesia/):
  - Indonesia carries no asterisk, so no Payoneer is needed and an ordinary Indonesian bank account works.
  - Requirements: a residential address, a bank account in an eligible country, and a valid credit, debit or prepaid card on file.
  - Etsy cannot pay out to PayPal or to a card.
  - Choose Indonesia as the bank country carefully; it cannot be changed later outside the Eurozone.
  - Identity verification may require government photo ID with a Latin-character name that matches the shop details.
  - A shop opened after 1 March 2022 can add its bank account after the first sale.
  - New sellers' funds are generally available 14 days after a sale. The default payout is weekly on Mondays.
  - Daily deposits require a balance of at least IDR 1,400,000.
- **Etsy VAT and FX:** Indonesian shops are in the group where Etsy must charge VAT on applicable seller fees regardless of tax ID. Etsy sends monthly VAT invoices on the 1st. The currency conversion fee is 2.5% when the listing currency differs from the payment account currency. — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/sell-on-etsy-from-indonesia/); FX fee also stated by [WorldFirst](https://www.worldfirst.com/af/blog/marketplaces-and-platforms/etsy-payment-methods-seller-guide/)
- **Etsy transaction fee:** 6.5% on top of payment processing. — [Growtsy (2026)](https://www.growtsy.com/etsy-fees/payment-processing)
- **Etsy payout friction (unverified):** A Threads post by @jadisidehustle (2026) complains to @etsy that "the current payout system via @flip_id is causing serious issues for sellers in Indonesia." Only the post title/snippet was readable, so the details are unverified. It suggests Etsy's IDR payouts may be routed via Flip. — [Threads](https://www.threads.com/@jadisidehustle/post/DeOLT1-E7jG/etsy-etsy-support-etsy-indonesian-sellers-need-your-attention-the-current)
- **Etsy fees, sellers' view:** In a Facebook community summary, sellers complain that fees can take "like half" of a sale. This is anecdotal. — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/fb-answers/etsy-sellers-in-indonesia/)
- **Gumroad payouts:** Indonesia appears in Gumroad's bank-payout table with IDR as the payout currency. PayPal is used only "if bank payouts aren't supported in your country," with a 2% payout fee. PayPal payouts go out on Fridays. — [Gumroad Help: Getting paid](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid)
- **Gumroad, Indonesian user report (Nov 2024, older):** "I live in Indonesia. With PayPal removed, the only payout method left for me is bank deposit." — [Reddit r/ecommerce](https://www.reddit.com/r/ecommerce/comments/1gk5odz/gumroad_payout_issues/)
- **Gumroad, Payoneer/Wise:** Gumroad pays via PayPal or direct bank deposit only, not Payoneer or Wise. — [Grey blog](https://grey.co/blog/how-to-receive-payments-from-gumroad-as-an-international-creator)
- **Gumroad fees:**
  - 10% + $0.50 per direct sale, plus sales tax.
  - Card processing of 2.9% + $0.30 is excluded and charged on top.
  - Sales through Gumroad's Discover marketplace pay a flat 30%, which includes processing.
  - Direct sales drop to 5% + $0.50 after $20,000 in paid sales in a calendar month. — [Gumroad Help: Gumroad's fees](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees)
- **Gumroad payout minimum:** scheduled payouts have a standard $100 minimum (third-party summary, 2026). — [InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/en/answers/does-gumroad-pay-via-paypal)

### Inferences
- **Etsy example:** a US$5 digital download on Etsy, priced in USD with an IDR payout, loses about:
  - 6.5% transaction fee
  - 4.5% + Rp7.000 (about US$0.43) processing
  - 2.5% FX conversion
  - VAT on those fees
  - the listing fee (not verified in this pass)
  
  That is roughly 22-25% or more, before ads. Pricing listings in IDR avoids the 2.5% FX fee but may look odd to buyers. Batching payouts above Rp1,4 juta avoids the Rp28.000 deposit fee.
- **Gumroad example:** on a US$5 product, the fixed $0.50 + $0.30 fees make small tickets expensive (about 26% total). Gumroad works better for US$15+ products. Discover sales cost 30%.
- Wise and Payoneer matter mainly for other platforms (e.g., Payhip and Lemon Squeezy, not covered here). For Etsy and Gumroad, direct IDR bank payout is the main route.

### Gaps
- I did not verify Etsy's current (post-Oct 2025) Payments Policy text directly. Only the old policy and the current help-page snippet were seen; figures appear unchanged.
- I did not verify the Etsy listing fee ($0.20) or Etsy's 2026 identity/registration hurdles specific to Indonesia (e.g., reports of new-shop rejections).
- The details of the Flip payout complaint, and whether Etsy uses Flip for IDR deposits, are unconfirmed.
- I found no data on how Gumroad converts USD to IDR, or its FX spread.

## 4. Indonesian tax obligations for online digital income (summary level)

### Takeaway
PPh Final UMKM at 0.5% of gross turnover remains permanent under PP 20/2026 (effective 22 April 2026, amending PP 55/2022). It keeps the Rp4,8 miliar ceiling, and individuals still get the first Rp500 juta of annual turnover tax-free. However, PP 20/2026 explicitly treats online content creators (influencers, selebgram, bloggers, vloggers) as "pekerjaan bebas" (freelance work), which is excluded from 0.5%. They must use NPPN (deemed-profit norm) or full bookkeeping with progressive Pasal 17 rates, and the relevant threshold becomes PTKP of Rp54 juta. Whether *selling digital product files* counts as trade (eligible for 0.5%) or creator/freelance income is a key ambiguity.

### Cited Findings
- **PP 20/2026 basics:**
  - The 0.5% PPh Final for UMKM is now permanent for UMKM with turnover up to Rp4,8 miliar.
  - The previous 7-year time limit under PP 55/2022 is removed.
  - Micro businesses with turnover up to Rp500 juta/year stay exempt.
  - Kementerian UMKM press release, June 2026. — [umkm.go.id](https://umkm.go.id/news/hrbsepuznsigv7yhhguv7j0j)
- **Eligibility and the creator exclusion:**
  - PP 20/2026 revises PP 55/2022, effective 22 April 2026. Eligible subjects are narrowed to orang pribadi, PT Perorangan and koperasi.
  - DJP says PP 20/2026 explicitly classifies online content creators (influencer, selebgram, blogger, vlogger) as pekerjaan bebas, which is not an object of PPh Final UMKM.
  - Other excluded professions include seniman, model, pengajar and penerjemah.
  - The Rp500 juta exemption belongs only to the 0.5% scheme. Outside it, PTKP applies (Rp54 juta/year for 2026). — [Taalenta](https://taalenta.id/blog/pajak-penghasilan-kreator-platform-luar-negeri/), citing [DJP article, 3 June 2026](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/artikel/era-baru-pph-final-umkm-bentuk-nyata-insentif-tepat-sasaran) and [IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/en/influencer-hingga-selebgram-resmi-tak-bisa-gunakan-skema-pph-final-05/)
- **Article 56 and progressive rates:** Article 56 excludes "content creators for online media (influencers, Instagram celebrities, bloggers, vloggers, and the like)." Excluded creators use NPPN (if turnover is at most Rp4,8 miliar) or bookkeeping. Progressive rates:
  - 5% up to Rp60 juta
  - 15% from Rp60 to 250 juta
  - 25% from Rp250 to 500 juta
  - 30% from Rp500 juta to Rp5 miliar
  - 35% above that
  
  The Rp500 juta tax-free facility remains for individuals who qualify as regular UMKM (not freelancers). — [SWS Consulting (Sept 2026)](https://swsconsulting.co.id/content-creator-dan-influencer-dicoret-dari-pph-final-05-di-2026-penjelasan-lengkap-pp-20-2026-dan-cara-hitung-pajak-baru/)
- **NPPN deadline:** Notice of using NPPN must be filed within 3 months from the start of the tax year (PER-17/PJ/2015). Without it, the taxpayer is deemed to choose bookkeeping for the full year. — [Taalenta](https://taalenta.id/blog/pajak-penghasilan-kreator-platform-luar-negeri/)
- **Foreign-source income:**
  - It counts toward the Rp4,8 miliar ceiling.
  - US platforms withhold 30% on royalties without a W-8BEN. With a W-8BEN claiming the US-Indonesia tax treaty, the rate drops to 10% (per the renegotiated row in DJP's treaty table).
  - Foreign tax paid can be credited under PPh Pasal 24 (PMK 192/PMK.03/2018), per country, limited to the lowest of three amounts. — [Taalenta](https://taalenta.id/blog/pajak-penghasilan-kreator-platform-luar-negeri/)
- **Marketplace withholding:** From 1 Nov 2026, marketplaces collect PPh 22 at 0.5% of seller turnover (PMK 37/2025). Sellers with turnover up to Rp500 juta can be exempted via a statement letter. Marketplaces may also start withholding automatically once turnover passes Rp500 juta. — [SWS Consulting](https://swsconsulting.co.id/pajak-marketplace-mulai-1-november-2026-tarif-05-siapa-yang-kena-dan-cara-kerjanya/); [Ortax](https://ortax.org/omzet-tembus-rp-500-juta-marketplace-bisa-otomatis-potong-pajak-pedagang-0-5-persen); [Pajakku](https://pajakku.com/artikel/umkm-online-wajib-tahu-cara-mendapat-fasilitas-bebas-pajak-untuk-omzet-di-bawah-rp500-juta)
- **Calculation example:** under the 0.5% scheme, cumulative turnover of Rp560 juta by July means Rp60 juta is taxable, giving Rp300.000 PPh. — [OnlinePajak](https://www.online-pajak.com/tentang-pph-final/pph-final-umkm-2026-diperpanjang/)
- **PPN on platform fees:**
  - Etsy charges VAT (PPN) on its seller fees to Indonesian shops regardless of tax ID. — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/sell-on-etsy-from-indonesia/)
  - Lynk.id's plan prices exclude PPN. — [lynk.id/pricing](https://lynk.id/pricing)
- **Crowdfunding income:** DDTC News reminds creators earning crowdfunding/donations (e.g., via Trakteer) to pay tax. That article is older and undated in the snippet. — [DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/34273/kreator-berpenghasilan-dari-crowdfunding-diingatkan-bayar-pajak)

### Inferences
- A seller of template files on Etsy, Gumroad or Lynk is plausibly running a "usaha" (trade in digital goods) and may still qualify for 0.5% with the Rp500 juta exemption. If their income is mainly from personal-brand content (endorsements, Trakteer donations, AdSense), PP 20/2026 pushes them to NPPN/progressive rates. Sellers should get a consultant's view, because this classification drives the effective tax a lot.
- **NPWP:** Indonesian NIK now functions as NPWP for individuals; this point is general knowledge and not re-verified here. Practical steps: keep records of omzet by platform, file the annual SPT (now via Coretax), submit W-8BEN on US platforms, and keep Etsy's monthly VAT invoices.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative DJP ruling on whether selling downloadable templates/e-books counts as "pekerjaan bebas" or trade under PP 20/2026.
- I did not find the NPPN percentage that applies to digital product sellers or content creators.
- PPN PMSE (11%/12% VAT on foreign digital services consumed in Indonesia) mainly affects buyers and platforms. I found no source on whether Indonesian sellers must charge PPN on domestic digital sales below the PKP threshold (Rp4,8 miliar). I did not verify this.

## 5. Earning potential: global market (USD) vs domestic market (IDR)

### Takeaway
Global platforms let the same template sell at roughly 3-10x the Rupiah price, but fees are much higher: about 20-30% on Etsy or Gumroad versus about 3-10% on Lynk or Mayar. Global platforms also bring stiffer competition and payout friction. The domestic market has low fees and familiar payments (QRIS, e-wallets), but it is price-compressed (Rp5.000-Rp75.000 templates) and saturated by PLR resellers. I found no rigorous comparative earnings data.

### Cited Findings
- **Domestic price anchors:** Rp5.000 CV template on Lynk; Rp10.000-Rp149.000 on Tokopedia; Rp65.000 on Shopee; Rp39.000-Rp149.000 for wedding invitation websites. — [lynk.id/aestydigital](https://lynk.id/aestydigital/7jggdd0k40nr); [Tokopedia](https://www.tokopedia.com/find/template-cv?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=find); [Shopee](https://shopee.co.id/Template-cv-ats-template-cv-canva-profesional-Desain-CV-Menarik-Ats-Friendly-Curriculum-Vitae-i.1556886622.26387724607); [indoinvite.com](https://indoinvite.com/harga); [seremoni.id](https://seremoni.id/)
- **Domestic fees:** Lynk 3-5%; Mayar 3-4% + channel fee (which can be passed to the buyer); KaryaKarsa 10%; Trakteer 5%. — [lynk.id/pricing](https://lynk.id/pricing); [docs.mayar.id](https://docs.mayar.id/mor/fees); [KaryaKarsa Help](https://help.karyakarsa.com/panduan-kreator/kebijakan-mengenai-gajiandikaryakarsa); [Trakteer](https://trakteer.id/feature-and-pricing)
- **Global fees:**
  - Etsy: 6.5% + 4.5% + Rp7.000 + a possible 2.5% FX fee + VAT on fees.

### Inferences
These are illustrative and assume about Rp16.000/US$. The exchange rate was not sourced in this pass, so check the current rate.

| Scenario | Gross price | Approx. fees | Net to seller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic, Lynk Basic | Rp50.000 | ~5% | ~Rp47.500 |
| Global, Etsy (USD listing) | US$8 (~Rp128.000) | ~24% | ~US$6 (~Rp97.000) |

- **Break-even:** one Etsy sale is worth about 2 domestic sales, so global wins per unit. Domestic can win on volume and conversion, because buyers pay with QRIS/e-wallets and come from the creator's own Instagram/TikTok traffic.
- **Likely strategy:** sell IDR-priced, Indonesian-language products (CV/lamaran kerja for local job formats, undangan, modul ajar Kurikulum Merdeka, soal CPNS) domestically. Sell English-language, globally relevant templates (planners, Canva social templates, Notion) on Etsy or Gumroad, priced at US$5-20+ so fixed fees matter less.
- **Tax treatment is the same** in principle for both markets (foreign income counts toward turnover). US-source income may face withholding if W-8BEN is not filed, though this applies mainly to royalty-type platforms. Etsy and Gumroad sales are generally sales income, not royalties; I did not verify this.
